# Pulse Dispatch — phone knowledge base

## Who we are
- Pulse Dispatch is an AI receptionist for trades and small practices in Toronto and the GTA.
- It answers your business calls 24/7, texts you the job, and books the customer.
- We set it up and run it for you. There's no software to learn.
- We work across Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville and the rest of the GTA.
- Ivan runs Pulse Dispatch. You can reach him at ivan@pulsedispatchai.com.

## Who it's for
- Plumbers, HVAC, electricians, roofers, locksmiths and tree services.
- Solo lawyers, mostly family and criminal.
- Therapists and clinics, like physio, chiro and massage.
- Anyone who misses calls because their hands are full.

## What it does
- It picks up every call, day or night, in your business name.
- It takes the caller's name, number, address and what they need.
- You get a text and an email with the job, seconds after the call.
- The caller gets a text saying "we got your request."
- It can ring you first, and only answer if you miss it. No more voicemail.
- If a caller asks for you, it can put them through to your cell.
- It can answer only after hours, if you prefer.

## Booking
- Two ways to book. It can book an open slot in your calendar during the call.
- Or it texts you the job, you pick the time, and you text the customer back from your own phone, the same number they called. No app needed.
- If the customer wants another time, they just text back. You confirm it.
- The AI never decides your schedule. You stay in charge.

## Short answers to common questions
- **Is there a contract?** No. Month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.
- **What if I go over my calls?** We text you first. Nothing gets cut off, and no surprise charges.
- **What counts as a call?** A real conversation. Spam, robocalls and quick hang-ups don't count.
- **Can I keep my number?** Yes. With Rogers, Bell, Telus or Freedom, you just forward missed calls to us. It takes two minutes, and your number stays yours. Or you can get a new local number.
- **How fast can I start?** Solo takes about 3 business days. Pro takes about a week. Team takes 2 to 3 weeks. Business takes about a month.
- **Do I need an app?** No. Everything comes by text and email.
- **Will callers know it's AI?** It sounds natural. If someone asks, it says it's the virtual receptionist.
- **Is there a free trial?** You're trying it right now. This call is the demo.
- **What about emergencies at 2 am?** On Team, real emergencies ring your on-call tech. On other plans, you get an urgent text right away.
- **Does it give legal or medical advice?** Never. It only takes details and books.
- **Is it cheaper than a receptionist?** Much cheaper. A full-time receptionist costs over 3,000 dollars a month. Pro is 299.
