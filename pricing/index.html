What the setup fee pays for.

The receptionist is built for your business, not copied from a template. The fee grows with how much there is to connect and test.

  • Solo · $49

    Your greeting, your service area and FAQs, call forwarding from your number, and a round of test calls before it goes live.

  • Pro · $99

    Everything in Solo, plus your techs and dispatch link, calendar connection, missed-call text back, web-form follow-up and review requests.

  • Team · $2,500

    A full call-flow map of how your shop runs, emergency and routing rules for each tech, your price list, one job-software connection, and two weeks of tuning on real calls.

  • Business · $6,000

    A written scope, CRM and calendar integrations by our developer, outbound campaigns, languages and custom voice, a dashboard, staff training and a 25 to 30 day build with test calls at every stage.

Billing, plainly.

  • Pay yearly, get 2 months free

    Solo is $1,290 a year instead of $1,548. Pro is $2,990 instead of $3,588.

  • Going over your calls

    We text you at 80% and at 100% of your calls. Nothing is cut off and there are no surprise charges. If it keeps happening, we'll suggest the next plan and only move you if you say yes.

  • Your number

    Keep your number and forward calls to us at no cost, or move your number over for free. A toll-free number is $30 a month.

  • Cancelling

    Month to month. Give 30 days' notice by text or email and that's it. Your number stays yours.

Pricing questions.

How much is an AI receptionist per month in Canada?

With Pulse Dispatch, an AI receptionist costs $129 a month for a one-person shop (100 calls), $299 for an owner-operator who dispatches jobs (300 calls), $599 for a crew of 2 to 10 (600 calls), and $1,000 for a multi-location business with CRM integrations. Prices are in CAD, plus HST, with a one-time setup fee from $49.

What counts as a call?

A call the AI answers and talks through with a real person. Spam, robocalls, wrong numbers and hang-ups under 10 seconds don't count. Texts, chats and web-form leads don't count either.

Which plan should I start with?

If you just want every call answered and the details texted to you, start with Solo. If you want customers to get a tech and arrival time by text, and web leads called back, start with Pro. Most solo trades start on Pro.

Is there a free trial?

You can hear exactly how it handles a call for free: ring (437) 476-0920 and talk to it like a customer would. Plans are month to month, so the most you commit to is one month plus the setup fee.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, any time. Moving up starts on your next bill; if the new plan needs extra setup (like a job-software connection), we only charge the difference between the setup fees.
