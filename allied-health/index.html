Health details stay off the phone.

Your receptionist is built to collect only what is needed to book: name, contact details, the reason for the visit in the patient's own words, and how they'll pay. It does not ask for diagnoses, history or clinical notes, and it says so if a caller starts to share them.

Call summaries go only to the people you choose. Ask us on the first call about where data is stored and how it fits your obligations under Ontario's PHIPA, and we'll walk you through it before anything goes live.

Questions from practices.

Can an AI receptionist book into Jane App?

It depends on your Jane plan and settings. Where direct booking is available, the AI books the open slot. Where it isn't, the AI takes the request, texts it to your front desk, and you confirm the time with the patient by text. We check your setup on the first call.

What does it cost for a solo therapist?

The Practitioner plan is $299 a month plus a $499 one-time setup, for up to 300 calls. If you only need calls answered and messages texted to you, the $129 Solo plan also works for a practice.

What happens if someone calls in crisis?

The AI is set up to recognise a crisis, tell the caller to call 911 or 988 right away, and alert you immediately. It never tries to counsel anyone.

Will patients know it's not a person?

It speaks in your practice's name and says it's the virtual receptionist if asked. Patients mostly care that someone answered and booked them.
