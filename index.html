Straight answers.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is a voice assistant that answers your business phone, talks with callers in plain English, takes their name, number, address and what they need, and texts it all to you. Pulse Dispatch builds it around your business and runs it for you, so there is no software to learn.

How much does an AI receptionist cost in Toronto?

Pulse Dispatch starts at $129 a month plus a $49 setup fee for 100 calls. Pro is $299 a month ($99 setup), Team is $599 ($2,500 setup) and Business is $1,000 ($6,000 setup). Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus HST. A full-time receptionist at Ontario's minimum wage costs over $3,000 a month.

Can I keep my business number?

Yes. Most owners keep their number and forward unanswered calls to us, which takes two minutes on any carrier. You can also move your number over to us, or get a new local 416, 647 or 437 number.

Can it ring me first?

Yes. Your phone rings first. If you don't pick up within the time you choose, the AI answers instead of voicemail. If a caller asks for you, it can put them through to your cell.

Does it book appointments by itself?

Your choice. It can book an open slot in your calendar during the call. Or it texts you the job, you tap the tech and time, and the customer gets a text with both. If they reply asking for another time, you approve it. The AI passes messages; you make the decisions.

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and speaks in your business name. If a caller asks, it says it's the virtual receptionist for your business. Most callers just want someone to answer and take the job.

How fast can I go live?

Solo is live in about 3 business days, Pro in about a week, Team in 2 to 3 weeks, and Business in 25 to 30 days. See every step from sign-up to go-live.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. If you go over your calls, we text you first. There are no surprise charges.
